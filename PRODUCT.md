## What this is

HelloVoice is a film and media production company in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. It makes commercial
video: shoots, edits, animates, and delivers finished films for brands operating in the Gulf.

**Positioning: healthcare and pharma specialist.** Roughly two thirds of the work is healthcare
and pharma. This is the wedge — the site leads with it rather than presenting a generalist
production company that happens to take medical clients.

English only. Arabic was considered and explicitly ruled out.

---

## The site's job

Two jobs, both weighted equally:

1. **Credibility for outbound and referrals.** Someone has been told about HelloVoice, or has
   been pitched by them, and is checking whether the work is real. The site has to survive that
   inspection in under a minute.
2. **Inbound lead generation.** Someone searching for a production partner in the region lands
   cold and needs enough to make contact.

Everything on the site serves one of those two. Nothing serves neither.

---

## Audience

Marketing leads, brand managers and agency producers at multinationals with a Gulf presence —
particularly pharma. They are commissioning film, they have a budget, and they are comparing two
or three studios. They are not filmmakers; they judge on evidence, not craft vocabulary.

## Constraints

- **Static site.** No framework, no CMS, no runtime dependencies beyond the animation stack.
  Generated by Python from `capture/content.json` and `build/content.py`.
- **Films play through Vimeo's own player**, opened on click. Nothing loads a player until
  someone presses play.
- **Posters are the branded ones from the current site**, at their native aspect ratios — nine
  of the 42 are portrait 9:16 and must not be cropped.
- **The hero reserves a slot for a 3D character** that tracks the cursor. The asset is a scrubbed
  video, dropped in when the client finishes generating it.
