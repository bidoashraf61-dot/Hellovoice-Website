How a campaign runs

FIVE STAGES
  1. Step 01

    Casting

    Shortlisted against the brief — audience, category fit and past brand work, not follower count alone.

  2. Step 02

    Brief & script

    A per-creator brief that protects the claim set and still sounds like them.

  3. Step 03

    Production

    Shot by the creator or by our crew, to the same spec either way.

  4. Step 04

    Compliance

    Every claim checked before it posts — the pharma work leaves no room for a re-cut after the fact.

  5. Step 05

    Delivery & reporting

    Master files, cutdowns and a post-by-post performance read.
